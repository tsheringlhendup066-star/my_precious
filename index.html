For My Brother & Sonam

This little page is for two people who are very far away from me...

But somehow, still very close to my heart.

😂

To My Brother...

Or should I say...

Water Pumpkin 🎃

I don't even remember exactly when I started calling you that.

But somehow that name became one of those stupid little memories that stayed with me.

Some Memories I Still Remember 😂

🐔 The way you ran from that chicken when you were little... honestly looked like something straight out of a cartoon.
🤦‍♂️ The way you can still be completely clueless sometimes.
📱 Watching those random funny videos on Facebook together thinking they weren't using much data.
😂 And somehow convincing ourselves that everything was fine.
🌸

And Then There's You, Sonam ❤️

I never really gave you a nickname... so I guess Sonam will have to do.

But honestly, I don't think you needed one.

Because the way you care, the way you love, and the way you look after the people around you already says enough about who you are.

❤️

Thank You, Sonam

I want to thank you for something that means more to me than I can probably explain.

Thank you for showing my brother how to love again.

Thank you for giving him someone who cares for him, loves him, and stands beside him.

😂❤️

You Two Are Perfectly Funny

One of you is so thin, the wind could probably carry him away.

And the other one is just chubby enough to hold him when the wind comes. 😂

So I guess you two were basically designed to balance each other.

🎁

Before You Leave...

There's one more thing I want you both to know.

No matter how far away I am, please remember that you both have a brother who loves you.
❤️

To Both of You...

Even though the distance between us is great, I still miss you both the same.

Sometimes, the pain of not getting to see both of you smile in the morning is really painful.

I miss the little moments. I miss being around you. And I miss knowing that you're both close.

But no matter what happens, I am still both of your brother.

And I will always be there to support both of you.

Even if someday it feels like it's me against the world, I will still stand beside you.

Please don't fight.

Stay happy. Stay loving. Take care of each other.

I love you both, and I always will.

❤️ Your Brother
